    03.About

    I am at the start of my testing career, so I want to be clear about what this is. These are practice projects on public demo sites and public apps. I did not test a company’s own product and I did not use test automation. Every report has the steps, the expected result, the actual result and a section that says what I did not cover.

    My work so far:
    • 12 reports and tables, with screenshots as proof
    • 23 SauceDemo test cases written as a test case table
    • 3 Android apps tested by hand on my phone
    • 7 bugs written as Jira tickets
    How I test:

    I explore first, then try the normal flow and then wrong or unusual input like empty fields, zero, negative numbers, very long text and spaces. On a phone I also try turning the phone, airplane mode and the Back button. When I have no requirement to compare with, I write an observation and not a bug.

    How I use AI:

    I use AI tools such as Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT as a helper. I run every test myself and take the screenshots. AI helps me with ideas for extra things to try, the wording of my reports, making the PDF files, checking my reports for mistakes and explaining things I do not know yet.
